- Run details show the electricity, heat and COP of the selected run (#327), integrated from the recorder history of its window: electricity from `power` (every member of a sum at the same time, else the power of the `units`), heat from the units' pairs, `heat`, or flow rate × spread (marked ≈). Each value needs valid data for at least 95 % of the run, the COP both; a running unit's heat counts for 10 minutes after it was written. Energy counters and hourly statistics are not split onto a run. Heat drawn during a defrost counts as negative.
