- Combined (layered) storage (#50): `storage: combined`, also in the editor, draws one tall tank with the hot-water zone on top and the heating zone below instead of a hot-water tank and a buffer. The six storage keys (`dhw_top` to `buffer_bottom`) are heights in the one tank, top to bottom, and the tank colour runs over every sensor at its height. The diverter valve charges the top zone for hot water and the middle for heating, the circuits draw from the heating zone like from a buffer, heater rods and solar thermal sit in their zone. Not together with `series_buffer`; presets never set it. New demo setup "Combined storage".
