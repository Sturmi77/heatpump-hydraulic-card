- Pressure of the heating system at the heat pump (requested in the forum): new entity key `system_pressure`, drawn with a gauge at the return of the heat pump. The presets that report a water pressure (Home Comfort, ESPAltherma, Vaillant, HeishaMon, Wolf, Kronoterm, Stiebel ISG, THZ, ebusd, Open3E) map it there instead of as `hc_pressure` at heating circuit 1. The thresholds `hc_pressure_min`, `hc_pressure_crit` and `hc_pressure_max`, the tile `hc_pressure` and `actions.hc_pressure` apply to it. Your own `hc_pressure` stays at circuit 1 and, also as `null`, replaces the value of the preset as before.
