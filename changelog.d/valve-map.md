- New option `valve_map` (raw state of the `valve` entity → `dhw` or `heating`) for a diverter or zone valve that does not follow "on = hot water", requested in #109. Example for a valve that reports 1 for heating and 0 for hot water: `valve_map: { 0: dhw, 1: heating }`. States outside the map keep the default reading, and the map applies to the timeline that is drawn from the compressor and the valve as well.
