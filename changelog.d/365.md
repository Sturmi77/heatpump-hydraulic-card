- New option `run_signal` (`auto`, `units`, `power`) for installations whose status or compressor entity updates only hourly, such as an ebusd cascade (#365, #173). With `units` a run lasts while at least one unit is above 50 W, with `power` while `power` is above `power_threshold`; hot water comes from the valve while the heat pump runs, the operating mode decides only cooling and a utility lock, defrost comes from a `defrost` entity. Dips under 2 minutes stay inside a run, stretches under 1 minute are dropped. The timeline, the run details and the live state follow the same signal; without recorded power the timeline falls back to the compressor and the mode. The diagnostic report names the run source. `auto` keeps everything as before.
