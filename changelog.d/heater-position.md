- Heater rods at a sensor height: `dhw_heater_top`, `dhw_heater_middle`, `dhw_heater_bottom` and `buffer_heater_top`, `buffer_heater_middle`, `buffer_heater_bottom` draw one rod per height, so a tank can show a rod at the top, in the middle and at the bottom (large tanks often have three). The middle and bottom rods stand at the label of the sensor with the same name, which moves a little to the left; the top rods stay above the large top value. Where a pipe enters the right wall at that height (solar loop, fresh-water station, buffer return) the rod moves just below it. `dhw_heater` and `buffer_heater` keep their place. Every rod in the hot-water tank, and in a combined storage every rod, excludes its run time from the `dhw_loss` tile. Also in the editor.
