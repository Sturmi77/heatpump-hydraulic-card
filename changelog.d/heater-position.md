- Heater rods at a sensor height: `dhw_heater_top`, `dhw_heater_middle`, `dhw_heater_bottom` and `buffer_heater_top`, `buffer_heater_middle`, `buffer_heater_bottom` draw one rod per height, so a tank can show a rod at the top, in the middle and at the bottom (large tanks often have three). The middle and bottom rods stand beside the value of the sensor with the same name; the top rods stay above the large top value. Where a pipe enters the right wall at that height (solar loop, fresh-water station, buffer return) the rod moves just below it. `dhw_heater` and `buffer_heater` keep their place. Every rod in the hot-water tank, and in a combined storage every rod, excludes its run time from the `dhw_loss` tile. Also in the editor.
- Tank values without position words: the middle and bottom values of the hot-water tank and the buffer no longer say "middle" / "bottom"; each value stands at the height of its sensor.
- Heater rods in a tank are visible while they run: red like the hot-water phase of the timeline (colour role `hot`, before the orange of a hot tank), with a dark casing and rising heat waves in the same red, like the heat waves of the emitters.
