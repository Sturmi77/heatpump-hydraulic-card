- Run details under the timeline (`timeline_details`, default on): a tap on an operating period, Enter or the arrow keys select the run it belongs to, from compressor on to off, divided where heating, hot water and cooling change, so a hot-water charge inside a long run can be selected on its own. Defrost stays a phase of the period it interrupts. A panel shows its time and duration, the phases, whether it began before the timeline or still runs, and its place in a series of short runs with the pause before it ("run 3 since 21:00 · pause before 8 min"). Run boundaries are marked on the bar. Without a compressor entity or its speed, operating periods take the place of runs; the `wall` layout keeps one line (#300, #324, #325).
