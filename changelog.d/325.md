- Run details under the timeline (`timeline_details`, default on): a tap on an operating period, Enter or the arrow keys select the run it belongs to, from compressor on to off, with heating, hot water and defrost inside as its phases. A panel shows its time and duration, the phases, whether it began before the timeline or still runs, and its place in a series of short runs with the pause before it ("run 3 since 21:00 · pause before 8 min"). Run boundaries are marked on the bar. Without a compressor entity or its speed, operating periods take the place of runs; the `wall` layout keeps one line (#300, #324, #325).
