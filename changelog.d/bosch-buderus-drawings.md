- Outdoor-unit drawings for Bosch and Buderus: the `bosch` drawing now follows the Compress 5800i/6800i AW outdoor units more closely (white casing with rounded top corners, a round grille of four wide rings around a large closed disc on the left, a plain right side); it keeps its key, so the EMS-ESP preset and configurations with `unit_style: bosch` get the new drawing. New: `buderus` (Logatherm WLW MB AR, anthracite, a ring grille with a dark open centre), `buderus_compact` (Logatherm WLW166i), `buderus_tall` (WLW196i AR), `buderus_tall_indoor` (WLW196i IR), `buderus_tall_silent` (WLW196i AR S+), `bosch_compact` (Compress 3800i AW), `bosch_cs8800i` (Compress 8800i AW), `bosch_square` (Compress 5000 AW), `bosch_tall` (Compress 7001i AW) and `bosch_tall_silent` (Compress 7400i AW). `unit_size: large` draws the squarer casing of the 10 and 12 kW units for `bosch` and `buderus`, and the casing with two fans one above the other for `bosch_compact` and `buderus_compact`. All of them are simplified original drawings without logos or lettering. Where the front is closed or finely meshed, a ring of the grille turns with the fan.
- Bosch/Buderus Home Comfort preset: with integration 0.9.1 the preset reads the heat generator sensor (`<gateway>:heat_generator`) and selects the matching outdoor-unit drawing and size from the reported product names, families and power. A TP70 indoor unit (70 l parallel buffer cylinder) turns `buffer` on, and the WLW176i and WLW186i indoor units turn `primary_pump` on. These are defaults only: `unit_style`, `unit_size`, `buffer` and `primary_pump` in the card configuration always win, and without the sensor a gateway of the brand Buderus gets the `buderus` drawing, any other the `bosch` drawing as before. The diagnostic report names a buffer that comes from the heat generator.
- Bosch/Buderus Home Comfort preset: once enabled in the integration, the DHW heater (`SRC.CUHP.HP1.DHWHeater`), the circulation pump (`SRC.IntDHW.CircPumpStatus`) and SG Ready (`SRC.CUHP.SmartGridMode`: blocked, normal, preferred as boost, forced) are mapped. Additional values that report nothing (`unknown` or `unavailable`, for example the pool starts of an installation without a pool) are no longer listed in the details; the preset looks again every minute and adds them once they report.
