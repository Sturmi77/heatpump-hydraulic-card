- New entity key `pv_boost` for a PV surplus mode (a binary entity that is on, or a power above 0), requested in #108. The card shows a "PV surplus" chip, the timeline turns the heating periods in which the mode was active into their own kind "Heating · PV surplus", and the new tile `pv_boost_share` shows the share of the heating time in PV surplus mode for today, yesterday and the week from the recorder history. The Lambda preset maps the E-Manager setpoint `emgr_power_consumption_setpoint` (W, register 104), so Lambda installations with the E-Manager get it without configuration. Other systems set `entities: pv_boost`.
