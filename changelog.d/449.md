- Power at the heater rods (#449): a heater with a power entity (W or kW) shows its power in the drawing while it runs, at the rod in the tank and under the backup heater in the heat pump. Where a sensor value needs the place beside the rod, the power stands outside the tank; without room it is left out.
- Narrow cards (#395): below 500 px the secondary values (tank bottom and middle temperatures, flow rates, hot water target, circuit return, circulation, valve) stand where there is room for them instead of being left out. `narrow_values: true` keeps the listed values in any case as before; `narrow_values: false` leaves all of them out, as it was without the option.
