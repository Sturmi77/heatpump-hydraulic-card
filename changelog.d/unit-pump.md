- New option `unit_pump` (also in the editor, default from the preset): draws the heat pump's own water pump at the flow outlet inside the heat pump block. It turns while the flow rate is above 0, else with the heating or hot-water loop. A single direct circuit without a `hc_pump` sensor then shows no pump of its own. The HeishaMon preset turns it on, because the Aquarea water pump sits in the outdoor unit (monobloc) or the hydro module (split) and also runs for hot water.
