- Bosch/Buderus Home Comfort preset for integration 0.9.0 with the local access: the preset now also finds the readings only the local access provides (`<gateway>:local_only:<resource path>`) and the same resource paths in the cloud's form. With the primary flow sensor TC1 the heat pump's flow is TC1 and the supply temperature behind the buffer cylinder moves to the first circuit; without TC1 the mapping stays as before. Newly mapped: the refrigerant circuit status (defrost and passive defrost), the electric power as the sum of compressor and auxiliary heater, the auxiliary heater, the primary flow rate PC0, the circuit pump PC1, the current supply setpoint, the outdoor unit's fan, the air intake TL2 and the refrigerant circuit temperatures TR6, TR5, TR3, JR0 and JR1 in the details (#195).
- Bosch/Buderus Home Comfort preset: an installation with the local access alone, without the cloud, is now recognised through its heat pump type or refrigerant circuit status; its operating mode then comes from the refrigerant circuit status. This is checked with synthetic data only.
- Bosch/Buderus Home Comfort preset: with the local access the controller signals add the compressor, the hot water 3-way valve VW1, the compressor's heat output where the unit's software provides it (live COP), the flow rate of circuit 1 (PC1) and the expansion valve VR1; the relay of the primary pump PC0 and the 4-way valve VR4 appear in the details. Resource paths stay first where one exists, so a signal the gateway stops listing does not stop a heat pump that draws power.
