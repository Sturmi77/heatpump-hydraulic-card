- Standing pipes keep more of their colour (#501): 32 % on light cards and 35 % on dark ones instead of 22 %, from the dark mode Home Assistant reports with `theme: auto`; `theme: night` uses 35 %, `theme: contrast` 32 %, and without a reported dark mode it stays at 22 %. A theme or card-mod can set its own share with `--hfc-idle-mix`.
